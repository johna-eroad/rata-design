# Vehicle and Driver

A [product segment](README.md). Read this when a task sits in Vehicle and Driver, to understand what the segment is for and to frame trade-offs against its north star.

## Value statement
Seamlessly connect every driver and vehicle to your fleet ecosystem with a flexible, device-agnostic platform spanning in-cab hardware, apps and OEM integrations. It delivers a superior in-cab experience while ensuring every asset, regardless of make or connection method, is visible, manageable and productive.

## North star
To seamlessly bridge the physical and digital worlds of every driver and vehicle, delivering a best-in-class connected experience that is device-agnostic, intuitive and reliable, so that no asset or driver is ever outside the ecosystem, regardless of how they connect.

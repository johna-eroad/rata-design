# Data and Intelligence

A [product segment](README.md). Read this when a task sits in Data and Intelligence, to understand what the segment is for and to frame trade-offs against its north star.

## Value statement
Unlock the full value of your fleet data with an open, connected intelligence platform that seamlessly integrates first and third-party data, delivers actionable network insights and powers a rich partner ecosystem, turning raw data into strategic decisions that drive competitive advantage.

## North star
To be the trusted data intelligence layer of the fleet industry, connecting ecosystems, surfacing insights and empowering operators and partners to make decisions that create lasting competitive advantage from the wealth of data their fleets generate.

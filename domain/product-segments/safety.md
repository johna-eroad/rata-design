# Safety

A [product segment](README.md). Read this when a task sits in Safety, to understand what the segment is for and to frame trade-offs against its north star.

## Value statement
Protect your drivers, reduce risk and lower operating costs with an integrated fleet safety platform that monitors behaviour, captures critical incidents and guides your heavy vehicles on the safest routes, turning data into actionable coaching that prevents accidents before they happen.

## North star
To eliminate preventable accidents and protect every driver on every journey, creating a world where data-driven coaching, intelligent monitoring and proactive risk management make fleet operations safer for drivers, passengers and the communities they travel through.

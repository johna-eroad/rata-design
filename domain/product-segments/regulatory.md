# Regulatory

A [product segment](README.md). Read this when a task sits in Regulatory, to understand what the segment is for and to frame trade-offs against its north star.

## Value statement
Simplify compliance across every region and regulation with an integrated platform that automates hours of service, vehicle inspections, weight management, tax reporting and fuel tracking, eliminating manual paperwork, reducing violation risk and keeping your fleet audit-ready at all times.

## North star
To make fleet compliance effortless and invisible, freeing operators from the burden of ever-changing regulations so they can focus on running their business with complete confidence that they are always audit-ready, wherever they operate.

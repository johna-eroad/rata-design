## Value statement
The powerful, intuitive foundation of your entire fleet operation. EROAD's core platform delivers real-time visibility of every asset on a live map, seamless driver and vehicle management, intelligent geofencing and instant communications, providing the connected, always-on backbone upon which every other capability is built.

## North star
To be the indispensable operational backbone of every fleet, where real-time visibility, seamless connectivity and intuitive management empower every fleet operator to make faster, smarter decisions every single day.

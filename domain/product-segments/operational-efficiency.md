# Operational Efficiency

A [product segment](README.md). Read this when a task sits in Operational Efficiency, to understand what the segment is for and to frame trade-offs against its north star.

## Value statement
Maximise fleet uptime and profitability with an integrated operations platform that optimises routes, jobs and loads, automates maintenance scheduling, monitors trailer and temperature conditions in real time, and delivers deep engine and fuel analytics, so you can do more with every asset, every day.

## North star
To unlock the maximum productive potential of every fleet asset, driver and load, where intelligent automation, real-time monitoring and deep analytics eliminate waste, reduce downtime and drive measurable profitability across every operation.
